### 2. Daily Closing Report Par Advance Booking Ka Impact

* **File:** [`app/api/reports/daily-closing/route.ts`](file:///d:/PGS-IMS-main/app/api/reports/daily-closing/route.ts#L33-L146)

#### **Day 1: Advance Booking Banane Wale Din**
1. **Cash Drawer (`cashReceived`):**
   * Jinne advance paise aaye (e.g. Rs. 10,000), wo aaj ke **Cash Received / Card / Bank** me shamil ho jatay hain (Line 41-44, 138-146). Is se counter cash drawer hamesha 100% match rehta hai.
2. **Customer Advances Liability (`customerAdvances`):**
   * Advance deposit dukaan par amanat (liability) hota hai, is liye report me **`Customer Advances`** me ye Rs. 10,000 add ho jata hai (Line 219-245).
3. **Total Sales, COGS & Profit:**
   * Report sirf `status: "COMPLETED"` wali sales ko revenue/profit me count karti hai (Line 35).
   * Is liye Booking wale din **Total Sales, COGS, Stock-Out Value aur Gross Profit me ZERO add hota hai**, kyun ke maal abhi dukaan se bahar nahi gaya aur sale fulfill nahi hui.

#### **Day 2: Customer ke Pickup / Completion Wale Din**
1. **Total Sales & Gross Profit:**
   * Jaise hi sale `COMPLETED` hoti hai, full item value (e.g. Rs. 50,000) aaj ke **`Total Sales`** me aati hai.
   * Realized Cost (`COGS`) aur Realized Profit (`Gross Profit`) aaj ki closing report me credit hotay hain.
2. **Remaining Cash:**
   * Baqi payment (e.g. Rs. 40,000) aaj ke Cash Received me shamil hoti hai. Total collection (Day 1 ka Rs. 10k + Day 2 ka Rs. 40k = Rs. 50k) complete match ho jata hai.
3. **Customer Advances:**
   * Advance liability clear ho jati hai.
